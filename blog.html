Know your drinks

A quick, honest guide to all six Ithri drinks.

Tiger Nut Milk

Creamy, nutty and naturally sweet.

Tiger nuts are small tubers enjoyed across West Africa, and this is their milk: smooth, rich and plant-based, with a gentle nutty sweetness.

  • TasteCreamy, nutty, lightly sweet
  • ServeWell chilled, shaken first
  • Enjoy withBreakfast or a light snack

Sugarcane, Lemon & Ginger Juice

Sweet, zesty and warming.

Pressed sugarcane brightened with fresh lemon and a warming kick of ginger: naturally sweet with a sharp, clean finish.

  • TasteNaturally sweet, zesty, with a ginger warmth
  • ServeIce-cold
  • Enjoy withSpicy meals and hot afternoons

Pineapple Juice

Bright, tangy and golden.

Sunny pineapple flavour with a sweet-tart bite. Simple, tropical and easy to love.

  • TasteSweet-tart and tropical
  • ServeChilled or over ice
  • Enjoy withRice dishes, grills and brunch
Order Now

Watermelon Juice

Juicy, light and refreshing.

Watermelon is mostly water, which makes this one wonderfully light. It is gentle on the palate and made for hot days.

  • TasteMild, juicy and refreshing
  • ServeWell chilled
  • Enjoy withLight lunches and fruit platters
Order Now

Zobo Mix

Deep, tangy and bold.

Zobo is traditionally made from dried hibiscus (roselle) petals, which give it that rich ruby colour and tart, fruity flavour.

  • TasteTart, fruity, bold finish
  • ServeIce-cold
  • Enjoy withParty food and small chops
Order Now

Coconut Water

Light, clear and hydrating.

Coconut water is the clear liquid found inside young coconuts: light, subtly sweet and very easy to drink.

  • TasteLight and subtly sweet
  • ServeWell chilled
  • Enjoy withAfter exercise and hot days
