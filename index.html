Our Mission

Less waste. More value.

Every year, a large share of Nigeria's fruit is lost before it ever reaches a glass. Ithri Fresh Juice exists to change that: when fruit gets processed, farmers earn more, waste falls and jobs are created.

Farmers, cooperatives and distributors: partner with us. Let's reduce waste and create value together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

How long does the juice last?

Our juices are made without preservatives, so keep them chilled and enjoy them within a few days of purchase.

Do you add sugar or sweeteners?

No. The sweetness comes from the fruit itself.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Message us on WhatsApp with your location and we'll let you know the delivery options available to you.

Can I refrigerate the juice?

Yes, please do. Fresh juice is best kept refrigerated and served chilled.
