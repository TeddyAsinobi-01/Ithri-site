Returns & Refunds

Fresh drinks, fair fixes

Our drinks are fresh and perishable, so the rules are short and simple.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

We will make it right if

  • A bottle arrives damaged or leaking
  • An item is wrong or missing
  • A drink arrives spoilt

We cannot take back

  • Opened bottles you simply changed your mind about
  • Drinks left unrefrigerated after delivery
  • Problems reported late

How to report a problem

  1. Tell us fast

    Message us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery.

  2. Show us

    Send a clear photo of the bottle and the problem. Keep the bottle.

  3. We fix it

    You get a replacement or a refund, whichever you prefer.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to you the same way you paid, or as a replacement if you would rather have fresh drinks. We will tell you when to expect it.

Cancelling an order

You can cancel any time before we start preparing your order. Once your drinks are made fresh for you, we cannot cancel.

This policy sits alongside your rights under Nigerian consumer protection law. Nothing here takes those rights away.

Something not right?

Send us a message and a photo. We will sort it out.
