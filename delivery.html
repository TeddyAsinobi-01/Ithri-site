How delivery works

  1. Message us

    Tell us what you want and where you are.

  2. We confirm

    You get your total, delivery fee and expected time.

  3. We pack

    Your drinks are packed chilled and ready to go.

  4. You receive

    Check your cans, then pop them in the fridge.

Where we deliver

We are based in Rivers State. Send us your location on WhatsApp and we will confirm if we can reach you, and how.

Fees and timing

The fee depends on your location and the size of your order. We tell you the fee and an estimated time before you pay.

When it arrives

Please have someone ready to receive it, and refrigerate your drinks as soon as they arrive.

Missed delivery

If we cannot reach you, we will try again or rearrange with you. Extra delivery charges may apply, as fresh drinks cannot wait around.

Delivery question?

Message us your location and we will help.
